# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

People shaping an early software idea who want a concise, reviewable local
record before discussing it with collaborators.

## Product Purpose

IdeaProof turns a short idea description into a technical specification and a
sample NDA template, preserves revisions, and timestamps the exact
approved files. Success means a user can move from idea to verifiable package
without an account or hosted IdeaProof service.

## Positioning

A local-first path from an early idea to concise documents with independently
verifiable timestamps.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and trustworthy. Copy is direct about what proof and generated
legal templates can and cannot establish.

## Anti-references

Avoid speculative legal claims, crypto-style hype, decorative dashboards,
enterprise form complexity, and interfaces that imply files stay entirely
offline during OpenAI generation.

## Design Principles

- Make the next decision obvious.
- Keep idea-stage documents concise.
- Show exact versions before approval.
- Explain local storage and network boundaries plainly.
- Treat accessibility and keyboard operation as core behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion
support, descriptive labels, and responsive layouts that remain usable at
mobile widths and browser zoom.
